Connections · six

Six things that turned out to be the same question.

A jewellery gala, a classical Chinese catalogue and an agent pipeline do not belong to one career plan. They belong to a few recurring questions. Each connection below names its two ends, says what I think links them, and labels how well I can prove it.

How to read the labels
Demonstrated Shipped artefact or live system you can open and check.
Firsthand My own record or experience, not independently documented.
Interpretation A reading I am offering, not a fact the work establishes.
Unverified Claimed somewhere, not yet proved. Flagged, not hidden.
01

I stopped asking people what they like.

Pulse Instinct never asks a respondent to explain their taste. It records what they choose, what they reject, and how long they hesitate. GramLingo makes the same move with language: there is no placement test. The app is the test — mistakes do the routing.

02

Two ways to model a person.

BrainyBaobei computes a BaZi chart from a date, a time and a place, then hands that chart to a model as context. Pulse Instinct builds a preference profile from behaviour. One model is centuries old and deterministic; the other is a database that changes weekly. Both answer the same question: how much of a person can be described before they speak?

That these are the same question is my reading, not a claim the two products make. I built them years apart and did not notice the overlap until I put them side by side.

03

Ancient systems, modern interfaces.

ASOC began as a catalogue of Chinese intellectual heritage: sources, dates, attributions, cross-references. BrainyBaobei began as an annual almanac product. Neither is a museum page. Both take knowledge transmitted by book, memory and apprenticeship and give it an interface that can be searched, computed and answered.

04

The studio built its own workforce.

Pulse Branding stayed one person. It did not hire a delivery team; it built an agent pipeline — orchestration, code generation and review, local-model memory, deployment with independent checks — and then used that pipeline to ship the products on this site. The studio was the prototype for the company.

05

Sell before you stock. Serve before you staff.

Three recent builds share an operating shape. NeoLabCare takes pre-orders with zero inventory and a seven-day dispatch. Firefly Global books appointments and takes payment without an enquiry queue or a receptionist. The Robotist site answers inbound questions itself. In each case the operational core is the smallest one that can still serve demand.

The line I am not going to draw.

High jewellery in 2016, robotics export in 2026, classical Chinese texts in between. A tidy version of this would say one interest led to the next. It didn't. The Bulgari gala and the Robotist site are connected by a method — how people decide, how a claim gets verified — not by a plan. Where a link is a fact it is labelled demonstrated. Where it is my reading of my own life it is labelled interpretation. The page should show the difference instead of resolving it.
